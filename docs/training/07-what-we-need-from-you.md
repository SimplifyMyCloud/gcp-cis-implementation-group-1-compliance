# 7. What we need from you

---

## Three things

**1. Speak in safeguard numbers.**
Not "the bucket thing" — "3.3". It is how the finding survives the trip to management intact.

**2. Only tick what you verified in the estate.**
A merged PR that has not been applied and confirmed is not compliance. Verify, then tick.

**3. When you raise a fix, record the PR on the checklist line.**

```
- [ ] All currently public buckets remediated `PR #123 2026-08-21`
```

That moves the item from *outstanding with us* to *awaiting approval* — and the report starts aging it.
